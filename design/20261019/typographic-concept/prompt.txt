Create one original art-directed editorial 3D typographic sculpture for the Russian design studio НЕПРОСТО. This is a visual style exploration for its website. Landscape 4:3 image.

The idea is an architectural landscape made entirely of custom Cyrillic letterforms. Bold close composition, extraordinary folded and intersecting type, playful controlled asymmetry, sophisticated material contrasts. A richly dimensional typographic artwork with the character of a gallery sculpture or a contemporary design festival identity.

Use the exact Cyrillic letters of НЕПРОСТО arranged spatially in three offset tiers: НЕ / ПРО / СТО. They may partially overlap in perspective, but their anatomy should stay recognizable. Design the letters as thin sculptural walls, deep cutout slabs, folded sheet surfaces and extruded curved ribbons; vary their depth dramatically. Some letter strokes curve back behind their face, one O is a wide translucent lime ribbon loop with a surprising folded return, an ivory П is like a precise architectural portal, graphite Р cuts diagonally through the composition, C has a finely ribbed inner curve. Make clever negative spaces, interlocking arches and spatial relationships. Refined custom geometric typography, not default extruded Arial and not inflated balloon letters. One coherent composition, not scattered alphabet blocks. Controlled impossible spatial relationships are welcome. Small details should come from the letter construction and materials, not added gadgets.

Palette strictly: warm off-white #f6f6f1 background, ivory, graphite #191b18, acid lime #c9df36 and a little brushed silver. Alternate opaque matte ivory, satin graphite, finely perforated dark metal on a single side plane, and translucent frosted lime acrylic that casts coloured shadows onto adjacent white letters. The lime has luminous depth through transmitted light, never neon emission. Expressive, crisp but tactile.

Camera: close three-quarter view slightly from above, only mild perspective. Sculpture fills most of frame and some peripheral letter edges are cropped deliberately, as an editorial composition. No floor horizon, no pedestal. Warm clean studio background, dramatic but soft directional lighting, beautiful readable shadows, clear material differences, rich dark areas, sharp focus throughout, tasteful fine surface grain. Premium physically based 3D finish.

Avoid any printing machine, factory, rollers, switches, screws, packaging, products, icons, UI, captions, diagrams, rainbow palette, blue, clay-like toy blocks, plain flat wordmark, generic centered logo mockup, copied artwork, watermark. Focus entirely on typography, sculptural form, depth and light.
